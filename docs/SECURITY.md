# Security Policy

We follow a coordinated disclosure process for the SaludEduca Forms platform
(`imas.saludeduca.com`, `ibsalut.saludeduca.com`, and related API hosts).
If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please let us know as quickly as
possible so we can investigate and remediate it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

- **Primary contact:** security@saludeduca.com
- **Subject line:** Please prefix messages with `[SECURITY]`.
- **Content:** Include a concise summary, the affected environment or URL, the
  potential impact, and the exact steps or proof-of-concept required to
  reproduce the issue. Screenshots, console output, and HTTP transcripts are
  very helpful.

## What to expect

1. **Acknowledgement:** We aim to acknowledge new reports within 2 business
   days.
2. **Assessment:** We will review the report, reproduce the issue, and evaluate
   impact and remediation options.
3. **Remediation:** For valid issues, we will prioritize a fix according to
   severity and keep you updated on progress.
4. **Disclosure:** Please give us reasonable time to deploy a fix before you
   make details public. We will coordinate timing with you.

If we need additional information or cannot reproduce the issue, we will follow
up using the contact details you provide.

## Scope

Reports covering the following assets are in scope:

- Production deployments of the Forms applications
  (`imas.saludeduca.com`, `ibsalut.saludeduca.com`)
- Supporting APIs and services contained in this repository (`api-imas.saludeduca.com`,`api-ibsalut.saludeduca.com`)

## Out of scope

To keep both parties safe, please avoid:

- Denial-of-service or resource exhaustion attacks
- Physical security testing or social engineering
- Automated scanning that degrades service quality
- Accessing, modifying, or deleting data that you do not own

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that adheres to this
policy, respects user privacy, and gives us a reasonable opportunity to fix the
issue before disclosure.

Thank you for helping us keep our users safe.
This is the way.
